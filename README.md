## 🌟 Features

- **Time Tracking**  
  Easily log your hours worked daily. The interface is straightforward and user-friendly. Just click “Start” and then “Stop” when you finish working.

- **Overtime Calculation**  
  The app automatically calculates any overtime based on your working hours. You can set custom working hours and policies to ensure accurate calculations.

- **Break Management**  
  You can schedule breaks and the app will remind you, helping you stay productive throughout the day.

- **Detailed Reporting**  
  Generate reports to see your working patterns. You can download these reports as PDF or CSV files for sharing with managers or for your own records.

- **Customizable Settings**  
  Adjust the app to fit your needs. You can configure work hours, overtime rules, and notification preferences easily through the settings menu.

## 🔧 System Requirements

## 🎓 User Guide

1. **Creating a New Work Log**  
   Click on the "New Log" button on the home screen. Fill in the required fields, including start time, end time, and any breaks taken.

2. **Editing a Work Log**  
   Navigate to the log you want to edit. Click the "Edit" button, make your changes, and save.

3. **Viewing Reports**  
   Access the “Reports” section from the main menu. Choose the date range and type of report you want to generate. Click “Generate,” and you will see the report displayed on the screen.

4. **Setting Preferences**  
   Visit the settings menu by clicking on the gear icon. Here you can customize work hours, break times, and notify settings.

## 🗃️ Troubleshooting

- **Installation Issues**  
  If you experience issues processing the installer, ensure your OS is compatible. Check to see that you have administrator privileges to install software.

- **Performance Issues**  
  If the app runs slowly, check system resources. Closing other applications may help.

- **Logging Errors**  
  If the app does not record your time, confirm you are pressing the “Start” button correctly.
